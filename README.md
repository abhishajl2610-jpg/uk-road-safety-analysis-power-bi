# UK Road Safety Analysis Dashboard - 2024 (Power BI)

## Overview
An interactive Power BI dashboard that analyses UK road collisions,
casualties and vehicles in 2024. It helps identify when, where and under what conditions
collisions happen, and who is most affected.

## Key Insights
- Collisions are spread fairly evenly across the year, with slightly higher counts in May, June and October
- About 71% of collisions happened in daylight
- About 79% happened in fine weather with no high winds
- Most collisions happened on dry roads (72K), followed by wet or damp roads (25K)
- The 26-35 age group had the most casualties (25K), followed by 36-45 (20K)
- Cars were involved most often (126K of 184K vehicles), followed by pedal cycles (15K) and vans (11K)
- Slight injuries made up most casualties (about 79%), and fatal casualties were about 2%

Note: most collisions happen in good conditions, most likely because most
driving happens in those conditions, so this does not mean fine weather or
daylight is more dangerous.
